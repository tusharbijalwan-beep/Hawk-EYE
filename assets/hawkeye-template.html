Hawkeye View

Prepared for CEO · CBO · CPO · CXO As of 25 Sep 2026, based on live dashboard reads
Bottom line: Trading activity is softening in the near term — real WoW/DoD declines on the platform's two largest volume lines (spot-adjacent Trading, and Futures) — while Options keeps growing share and holding fee take-rate. That's masked in places by a genuinely broken data layer: one SQL bug is live identically across three dashboards, and two separate backend pipelines are down, so several product-health metrics (retention, acquisition, liquidation exposure) are currently unmeasurable, not just softer.

Five business lines were reviewed end-to-end across 11 dashboards: Options, Futures, Spot/Trading, Advisory (expert calls), and cross-platform Product engagement. The numbers below are what's actually rendering on each dashboard as of today — anything graphical-only or pipeline-broken is flagged rather than estimated.

Flags for leadership

6
Critical — Engineering

The same column bug is broken identically on three dashboards

order_exit_failure_perc cannot be resolved — Product dashboard, Daily Product Metrics, and Futures product Metrics all throw the same unresolved-column error on their Order Exit Failure % chart. One schema change, unfixed, silently blinding exit-failure visibility across both Options and Futures product lines.

Critical — Data infrastructure

Two separate backend pipelines are down right now

Cefi-Dev-DBT-Databricks is stalled across Options (Market Level Data, most of Retention), Futures Tracker (5 of 7 tabs — Acquisition, Retention, Resurrection, OKR View, Targets-Progress), and Futures product Metrics (liquidation & user-segmentation charts). Separately, Cefi-SQL-Endpoint-Analysis has taken down all of Daily Metrics' Resurrection, Retention, and AnA tabs. Between the two, leadership currently has no visibility into retention or resurrection health on either Options or Futures.

Caution — Vendor management

Three advisory suppliers are 0% approved and still on the roster

chahat@marketwind.in (54 calls, 100% rejected), rachit@marketwind.in (3 calls), and bitnomics.research@marketwind.in (3 calls) show zero approved picks. Separately, krishna@marketwind.in has the single best risk-reward ratio of any supplier (1.58) but the worst hit-rate (37.5%) and a 60% rejection rate — the setups look good on paper and fail in execution or approval. Worth a supplier-quality review before the next contract cycle.

Caution — Data quality

Read the "latest week" numbers with suspicion

Two independent dashboards (Daily Metrics' CLM tab, and Crypto Market Volume) show the current/most-recent week collapsing to near-zero across every metric and every exchange — including Binance's own spot volume dropping from $71.8B to $322M week-over-week, which isn't a real market move. This reads as a partial/in-progress data bucket, not a genuine crash. Don't quote current-week numbers from either dashboard without confirming the period is closed.

Watch — Engagement

Home Expert Picks engagement is fading

Home_CLM_experts_picks_clicked has fallen from ~5.5% of homepage clicks in early September to 2.7% in the latest week, while Portfolio_NAV_clicked has climbed to 17.7%. Worth a look at whether this is placement/algorithm-driven or genuine interest decline, given Expert Picks' role in the Advisory funnel.

Watch — Ownership

Advisory Business Metrics Dashboard hasn't been touched in 8 months

Data still appears to refresh, but the dashboard itself is stale on ownership/maintenance — worth confirming someone still owns this before leadership relies on it for Advisory's platform-contribution numbers (currently: 0.34% of platform volume, 3.8% of platform users, weekly).
